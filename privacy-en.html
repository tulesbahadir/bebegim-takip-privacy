Privacy Policy

Bebeğim Takip · Last updated: November 2026

Bebeğim Takip does not sell your or your baby's data and does not share it with third parties.

Information We Collect

Bebeğim Takip only stores the information you enter:

  • Account information: your email address
  • Baby name and parent name
  • Feeding records (amount, type, time)
  • Diaper records, including photos if you choose to add them
  • Sleep records
  • Growth measurements (weight, height, head circumference)
  • Vaccine and doctor appointment records
  • Notes, milestones and saved sound links
  • Family sharing: the name and email address of people who request to join your family

Where Is Your Data Stored?

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore. You can read Google's privacy policy here.

Who Do We Share Your Data With?

Your baby's records are visible only to you and to the family members you approve. We never share them with anyone else.

To measure our own advertising campaigns, the app uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK. It sends basic app events, such as app installs and app opens, to Meta. If you allow tracking, your device's advertising identifier is also shared with Meta. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. You can read Meta's privacy policy here.

We do not show ads in the app and we do not sell your data.

Data Security

Your data is protected with industry-standard security measures using Firebase's security infrastructure.

Children's Privacy

The app is designed to be used by parents. It is not intended to be used by children.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any of your records in the app at any time. You can also delete your account completely from Settings → Delete account, or by writing to tulesbahadir@gmail.com.

Changes to This Policy

When we update our privacy policy, we will update this page. We may notify you in the app about important changes.

Contact

For questions: tulesbahadir@gmail.com
